Policy
Returns & exchanges
30-day hassle-free returns — we collect from your door
1
Contact us
Email or WhatsApp us within 30 days. Include your order number.
2
We collect
Our team collects the item from your address. No packing needed.
3
Inspection
We inspect the item at our warehouse within 3 business days.
4
Refund issued
Full refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
What's not covered
Custom-made pieces are non-returnable unless defective. Items showing signs of use or damage beyond normal inspection are not eligible. For defective custom pieces, we will repair or replace at no cost.
